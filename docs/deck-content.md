## S4 — What is a GCC? (Global Capability Center)

An offshore/nearshore entity set up by a corporation to deliver specialized functions
— IT, Finance, HR, Analytics, R&D — with cost efficiency, talent depth, operational agility.

- **Cost Arbitrage** — World-class talent at 40–70% lower cost vs. home markets, no quality compromise.
- **Operational Excellence** — Dedicated teams on core processes — consistency, compliance, scalability.
- **Innovation Hub** — GCCs evolve into R&D/innovation centers driving product dev & digital transformation.
- **Strategic Control** — Unlike outsourcing, a GCC stays fully aligned with parent culture, goals, IP.

## S19 — Engagement Models ("Are You Ready?")

- **Project-Based** — fixed scope/timeline/price. For: website dev, compliance audit, recruitment drive, market research.
- **Retainer** — monthly, dedicated resources, ongoing ops. For: virtual assistant, finance & bookkeeping, IT support, HR & payroll.
- **Outcome-Based** — pay per result. For: medical billing (% collections), lead gen, recruitment (per hire), sales (commission).
